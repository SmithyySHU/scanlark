# Vector Trace Notes

The approved horizontal PNG was used as the sole shape source for the production
logo family.

The lark, magnifying glass and wordmark were separated by colour and position,
cleaned, smoothed and converted into path-only SVG data.

All horizontal, stacked, mark, dark, monochrome, favicon and print variants are
derived from those same paths.

This gives the logo unlimited SVG scaling and prevents separately generated
variants from drifting into different designs.

Because the source began as an AI-generated raster image, a professional brand
designer could still perform a final manual node clean-up before a formal
trademark filing or very large-format print run. The supplied SVGs are suitable
for normal website, email, report, social and general business use.
